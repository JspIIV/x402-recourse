The lifecycle

The seller is paid by default.
The buyer has one way back.

x402's auth-capture scheme holds a payment before it pays. In every operator type the spec defines, the seller's side decides capture or void. Its "custom" type allows arbitration. Recourse is that operator, with Internet Court deciding.

01 · THE 402

One signature

The buyer signs one EIP-3009 authorization. The request itself is committed into the payment's salt, so the record of what was asked is the buyer's own.

02 · THE HOLD

Coinbase's escrow

The money goes to AuthCaptureEscrow, unmodified. It can only be paid to the listed seller or returned to the buyer.

03 · THE WINDOW

Silence pays

The seller records its response on chain. If the buyer says nothing in the window, anybody captures the payment to the seller.

04 · THE COURT

A dispute is judged

Validators read the seller's offer, the request and the response. DELIVERED pays the seller, NOT_DELIVERED returns the money. The court sends it.

Internet Court as its operator.

The escrow and its collector are vendored from base/commerce-payments at a pinned commit and match Coinbase's own deployments on Base and Base Sepolia in every executable byte (scripts/verify_evm.mjs).
